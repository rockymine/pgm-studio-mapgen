# tanglewold — measured record

CTW, woodlands. `mirror_x`, cell 5, 20 players, board **170 × 105**. A forest board: meadow home ground,
two wools per team at opposite depths (a walled knoll room north, a **donut** hollow room south), a
causeway mid island reached over two 10-block fords, brooks fanned to both halves.

## What it exercises

- **The box model**: four boxes annotated (`wool` ×2, `hub`, `frontline`); the south wool's donut is
  *recognized* by the producibility read. The sanction that let it pass is a naming convention — the ring
  legs must share the room's id prefix (`wool-south-rim`, `wool-south-side`) — found the hard way
  (`wool-ringed-hole` fired against `south-rim`) and worth documenting (report fault 8).
- Forest as gameplay structure: 12 template oaks/birches + 2 `grown` trees (one dark-oak wood — the
  *template* dark_oak builds oak blocks, run-2 finding, so grown is the honest route), flora at fern-heavy
  coverage in the belts, boulders as mid-wood cover. Circulation (three dirt tracks) drawn first; every
  building 3+ from the spline.
- One `causeway` theme on the spine island (mossy-cobble voronoi over cobble) against three forest-floor
  noise themes and two perch themes; earthy `turbulence` walls with `rise` so banks don't stripe.
- Walls on **both** wools (bridgid-ii's one-wall-per-objective, copied).
