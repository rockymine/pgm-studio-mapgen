# Karnbeck — a core in a keep over a beck

**A wooded beck valley where each team's core stands in the yard of a ruined keep on a bluff at the front of
its half.** The beck winds across the valley floor below the bluff under a timber footbridge. A mill hamlet
stands round a mill pond behind the bluff, and oakwood and birch climb the valley's two sides.

Slug `opus55-karnbeck`, map name **Karnbeck**, built on the deployed studio. The author's questions on it are
notes 14–16 (`reports/opus55-notes-run.md`).

## How it is meant to play

**A core is breached where it stands, so it stands forward, where it is fought over.** It is 55 blocks from its
own spawn and 181 from the enemy's on the plan tier, a ratio of 3.29 against `GO1`'s 3.0–4.0. The halves meet
across a 32-block build zone over void the whole width.

**The attacker's last stretch is a climb out of a wet valley.** The beck is six wide on a floor at 12; the
bluff stands at 22 and is climbed by a graded way from the footbridge up through the keep's east gap.

**The keep's ring is four courses of stone twelve out from the core, open in three gaps.** The gaps face east
to the bridge, south-west and north-west, and a crenellated tower on the ring's north-east looks down the
valley. The lane through the north wood and the mill lane to the south are the ways round.

## What the ground is made of

**Meadow over dirt over grey rock, finished by angle.** Grass holds up to 34°, dirt and coarse dirt to 48°, and
stone and andesite above that. The keep's yard is a built floor of stone brick, polished andesite, andesite and
stone.

**The houses are white clay between dark-oak timbers under brick roofs,** forked from a timber cottage: a laid
dark-oak sill, dark-oak posts, no footing. The brick of the roofs is the board's accent. The keep is stone
brick with andesite, the one grey on the board.

## What went wrong

**The valley's sides were first pushed too steep,** and three oaks stood on bare rock; a falloff of 18–22 blocks
instead of 12 put grass back under them. The pushes also reached into the beck's course and cut a six-course
trench (`DR-BANK`) until they were moved west.

**The footbridge was first stated level with the valley floor and the water carved it away**, all but one
plank on the far bank; a course higher it stands over the beck.

**The flanks read 42% dead and drawing a lane does not change that.** Coverage counts only journeys between
spawns and goals, so on a one-core board the woods are ground nobody's journey passes. Note 16 asks the author
about it.
