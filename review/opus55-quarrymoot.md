# Quarrymoot — three hills in a quarry

**A King of the Hill board in a red-sandstone quarry.** The two spawns stand on the rim at either end and look
into a pit that steps down one bench to a floor. The centre hill is on the floor under a crusher house's roof,
the two flank hills on the bench across the line between the spawns, and a timber conveyor gantry crosses the
whole pit at rim height over the crusher.

Slug `opus55-quarrymoot`, map name **Quarrymoot**, built on the deployed studio. The author's questions on it
are notes 20–22 (`reports/opus55-notes-run.md`).

## How it is meant to play

**`match-flow.md` §10 is the law it is built to.** The ground is made rather than grown; no hill stands over the
ground round it; the centre pays two a second against the flanks' one; cover is placed in two sizes; and every
part of the board is a way toward a hill.

**The centre is the lowest point and roofed.** Its pad is on the floor at y13 under a stone roof at y20 on four
3 × 3 piers, open on every side, so nothing on the rim has a line onto it. The gantry at y22 over the roof is a
second storey over the same footprint.

**Four haul roads come down the benches, two from each spawn.** Each runs from the rim at 22 to the bench at 18
and on to the floor at 14, and the gantry joins the two flank benches over the centre.

**Cover is quarried blocks.** Small ones, two and three courses tall, stand about the floor and the benches;
one large stack stands beside each flank hill on its own team's side, gone round from two sides.

## What the ground is made of

**Red sand, red sandstone and orange clay in the pit, over banded red sandstone, orange clay and hardened
clay.** The beds follow the ground, so every bench face shows them. The rim is turf, which the Mesa biome tints
to meet the orange.

**The built family is grey and dark:** stone-brick works buildings and a stone crusher house, dark-oak gantry and
trestles. The haul roads are gravel, andesite and cobble.

## What went wrong

**One landmass may not mix a fanned piece with one that is not (`PL12`).** The quarry was first one centred
piece off the mirror; it is now each team's half as one piece, the two meeting on the axis.

**The gantry's trestles first stood inside the flank hills' pads (`SK18`)**, and the haul roads met the bench on
three-block seams (`RL3`) until they were widened. Both were fixed before the board was looked at in game.
