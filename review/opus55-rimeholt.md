# Rimeholt — a composed capture board in snow

**A capture board in snow and spruce.** Each team's hub is a snowfield leaning up from its frontline to a
timber lookout on a hamlet behind it, with a frozen tarn in its west corner and spruce fells off both its
coasts. A ring of standing stones stands on the mid stone in the build band.

The arrangement is composed board p12 t2 seed 7 (`composed-p12-seed7.plan.json`). One piece is added — the
hamlet, flush with the spawn — and nothing else about where the pieces stand is changed. Slug `opus55-rimeholt`,
map name **Rimeholt**, built on the deployed studio; the author's questions are notes 17–19.

## How it is meant to play

**Two wools a team, each at the end of a spur behind a bedrock wall.** The west spur leaves the hub's back
corner and the east one its middle, so the two ways out of the hub differ. The twin frontline prongs round the
composer's hole are left as composed, `FR9` and all.

**The hamlet gives the spawn a second door.** Joined to the spawn, the hamlet opens the room west onto it as
well as south onto the hub; the lookout stands in that lane.

## What the ground is made of

**Snow over packed ice, and rock only where the ground is too steep to hold snow.** Snow is the ground and
packed ice its patches; the tarn is packed ice set a course into the snow. Under the spruce the floor is podzol
and coarse dirt with snow at its edge.

**The built family is spruce log: the cabins, the lookout, the log piles and the timber halls the rooms are
stamped in.** The accent is grey: the standing stones and the gravel, cobble and andesite of the paths.

## What went wrong

**Snow is not soil, so a tree may stand on 37 cells of this board and no more.** The pass refuses a trunk on
anything but grass or dirt (`DR-ROOT`). The spurs and the hamlet are kept clear for doors and walls, so four
spruces tried there were declined. The board carries four spruces a team, on needle strips along the hub's
coasts.

**The east fell first lifted the ground under wool B's wall**, which then stood six courses over its lowest
column (`ST4`). Moving the fell off the spur put it back to four.
