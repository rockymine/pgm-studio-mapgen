# Marlbeck

The white paper's worked example. A destroy-the-monument board authored from nothing through the pgm-studio
HTTP API, in five revisions, with every finding it met recorded in `white-paper.md` §5.

```
84 × 256 blocks · rot_180 · 16 players a team · one monument a team
two halves joined only by a build zone over a 32-block strait
```

## What it is made of

**The plan** is two pieces, one zone and two markers — the *arrangement*, and nothing else. A piece added to
give a theme somewhere to hang is the failure this repository is named after, so the fell, the mere, the
shelf, the wood and the paint are all in the second document.

**The finish** is three relief marks and one push, three themes, three theme patches and fourteen props,
generated by `build-spec.py`:

| | |
|---|---|
| `fell` | a `scarp` down the east side — high 32, low 20, a 7-block face, an 8-block band held flat either side |
| `beck` | a `line` cutting the bed to 16, with 8 blocks of it held flat for the water to sit in |
| `shelf` | an `area` at 24 with a 5-block bevel — the ground the monument stands on |
| `west-rise` | a `push` of +7 behind the shelf, so the two ways past the monument are not the same walk |
| `moor` · `flash` · `workings` | 90.7% · 5.5% · 3.8% of the ground |

The ground theme's surface is a `layered` material on the **`slope`** axis: grass under 24°, coarse dirt from
24 to 40, stone above. One statement finishes the flat, the shoulder and the face of the same hill.

## What it ships with

One complaint, on every build:

> `DR-DRY` — water 'beck-water' stands against **122 open column(s)** of drawn ground — first at
> `(-42, 18, -58)`, where the basin is dug to the water's own depth and holds none.

Two documents describe one lake: a relief mark cuts the bed, a water prop fills it, and where the cut reaches
further than the fill, ground is excavated and never covered. Four attempts moved the count from 400 to 122;
the residue is at the board's own western edge, where the mark's band is clipped and the pool's end-disc does
not cover it. It is left in because the rule says the world is built and the water is in it, and because a
paper about a system that reports rather than nags should show one of its reports standing.

**Two things a reader should know about the board as a board.** Its 34.4% dead ground is a number its author
accepted, not one the studio approved — one monument a side gives no reason to walk to the flanks. And
`flash-w`, one of the three theme patches, ended up under the mere after the water was widened, so only one
of the two flash patches is visible from above. Neither is a fault the studio raises.

## Re-driving it

```bash
export PGM_STUDIO_API=http://localhost:7894/api
python3 build-spec.py                                     # writes the two documents
python3 ../../tools/drive.py . "Marlbeck" --out world --renders renders --slug marlbeck
```

`marlbeck.layout.json` and `marlbeck.intent.json` are what the last run *posted*, not inputs: the plan and the
finish are the authored pair, and the layout is compiled from the plan on every run.
