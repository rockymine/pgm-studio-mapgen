`id`, `type`, `operation`, `vertices`. No `floor` and no `base_height`, because a subtract has
no use for either — which is the same fact `height-not-read` reads out of the built world.

The names are `void-1-cut` and `void-2-cut`: `PlanVoids.Declare` names every gap between pieces
a `void-N` buffer on every compile, drawn or not, and each one compiles to a cut.
`techniques/taking-over-a-composed-board` is where the plan side of that is worked.
