### the built world, per variant: how many of each hole's 144 blocks came out as ground
    1-as-pinned              land  5372   hole-1   0/144   hole-2   0/144
    2-void-redrawn           land  5436   hole-1  32/144   hole-2   0/144
    3-a-surface-per-piece    land  5372   hole-1   0/144   hole-2   0/144
    4-taken-over             land  5145   hole-1  13/144   hole-2   0/144

`2-void-redrawn` has 32 blocks of ground in hole-1 because its SUBTRACT was redrawn with the
corners taken off. No add was written; the board simply states less negative space than it did.

`4-taken-over` has 13 there for a different reason, and it is the one worth knowing: the far bar
came out, so that void is no longer ENCLOSED. The compiler emits one cut instead of two and the
merged outline traces round what is left of the hole, keeping the corners the bar used to bound.
Taking a piece away changes which voids are holes, and the cuts change with it.
