The staircases walk. Four one-block risers between a bar at 9 and a bar at 13, and nothing
placed either way — which is the whole of what splitting a piece into treads buys.

**A build zone is crossed level and costs the blocks it costs.** Both lanes read `worst step 0`: the
bridge is levelled from the shore the player leaves, so it is flat until the far side, and the count of
blocks placed is the price. The mid band crosses the same way onto the holm at 8.

**The wall reads as `barrier +4` and that is the read being literal about walking.** A `walls` entry
stamps four courses of bedrock a defender can build on and cannot lose, and four courses is what an
attacker bridges — the barrier is the feature. `walk` prices a walk and has no word for it, which is
the author's reading of why so few maps have ever carried one: the read condemns them. Filed as
`WS69` and `WS70` in `pgm-studio`.

**The deck is a climb and that is the point of it.** Four courses to the lower floor and nine to the
roof, built up from the holm a player has just bridged to — a height nobody is given and anybody can
take.

**A prop leaning over a rim is what spoils these numbers, which is why no tree here stands on one.**
Three oaks put back on the lane's shore hang a leaf at y20 over the void; `column` and `transect` both
call that column void, and a walk to it answers reachable, `barrier +8`, standing at y21 and y22 in the
canopy on the way. Filed as `WS71`, with the question it turns on.
