The staircases walk. Four one-block risers between a bar at 9 and a bar at 13, and nothing placed
either way — which is the whole of what splitting a piece into treads buys.

**The wall reads as `barrier +4` and that is the read being literal about walking.** A `walls` entry
stamps four courses of bedrock a defender can build on and cannot lose, and four courses is what an
attacker bridges — the barrier is the feature. `walk` prices a walk and has no word for it, which is
the author's reading of why so few maps have ever carried one: the read condemns them. Filed as
`WS69` and `WS70` in `pgm-studio`.

**And the lane entered from the back bar reads `barrier +7`, which is a treetop.** The same crossing
from the front bar is level at 9 and costs three blocks. Three cells of the lane's north row carry a
crown over the void — `transect` along `z 43` reads `(-25, 43)` as `ground 20, standing tree` — and a
bridge is levelled from the nearest ground, so the canopy becomes the height the zone is crossed at.
Filed as `WS71`.
