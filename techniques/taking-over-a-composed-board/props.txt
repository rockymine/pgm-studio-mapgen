The last three are outside the field above, and that is the point of the distinction. The search
keeps a cell whose eight neighbours are ground at one height, so it refuses every RIM cell on the
board: beyond a rim is void, and a neighbour that is not ground fails the test. What the studio
asks is narrower — ground under the trunk, three clear of the paving, unclaimed, not kept clear —
and the bar's outer rim passes all four. A site is checked against the dressing pass; the search's
silence is conservatism and not a refusal.
