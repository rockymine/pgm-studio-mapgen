The two adds of `1-as-pinned` are one merged polygon per COMPONENT — the team unit and the
neutral mid — not one per board. A surface per piece ends that merge: nothing is left to merge
once no two touching pieces share a height, and `3-a-surface-per-piece` answers one polygon per
height, seven of them. What it does NOT end is the subtract — both void cuts survive, because
`PlanVoids` reads the void per component rather than per surface.

The answer is one polygon per height per CONNECTED REGION, and the taken-over board is where the
difference shows: nine heights come back as twelve polygons, because the staircase's treads put
10, 11 and 12 on two cross-pieces that do not touch. `hub-t1-10` and `hub-t1-10-2` are those two,
and `themeById` is what addresses one of them at a time.
