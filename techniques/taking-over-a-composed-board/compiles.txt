neutral mid — not one per board. A surface per piece ends the merge inside a component and
answers one polygon per height per connected region: `hub-t1-9`, `hub-t1-9-2`, `hub-t1-10` and so
on, eleven of them over six heights. What it does NOT end is the subtract: both void cuts are
still there, because `PlanVoids` reads the void per component rather than per surface.
