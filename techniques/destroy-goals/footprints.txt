A `pillar` is one column however tall it is. `cube-3` and `cube-4` are solid squares, and
`column-plus` is the one that is neither — five cells a layer with its corners left open, which
is why it reads as a cross rather than as a block.

An even-sided cube has no centre column, so a `cube-4`'s anchor sits on one of its four middle
cells rather than in the middle of the square. Where a goal has to be centred on something, that
is an offset to author rather than a fact to assume.
