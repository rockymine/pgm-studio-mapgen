GET …/walk — straight at the wall, at each of the three stations. A walk travels its aim line and
reports what it meets, so this is the cost of going THROUGH rather than the route a player finds.

Each line ends with `barrier +7` and a drop at the wool room's own wall, which every station has
and no defence wall put there. What the defence wall costs is the `barrier +4` before it: one at
`line` and `shoulder`, two at `sealed`.

### what a wall is, read at (8, 32)
  === column (8, 32) — 13 solid block(s), ground 0° from level ===
  y 12    30:0   Cobweb
  y 11     7:0   Bedrock
  y 10     7:0   Bedrock
  y  9     7:0   Bedrock
  y  8     7:0   Bedrock
  y  7     7:0   Bedrock
  y  6     7:0   Bedrock

A wall is bedrock, and nothing breaks it or is placed over it. That is the whole of the device:
the decision is whether a player meets it, and the geometry either side is what decides.

At `shoulder` two legs reach the approach behind the wall with nothing placed, because a piece of
ground was pulled out past each of the wall's ends. At `line` the same two columns are void —
there is no ground beside the approach, so the wall is the only way through.

`sealed` is the other bound: two `barrier +4` steps on one approach, which
`docs/gameplay/approaches.md` settles as a sealed room rather than a prepared line.
