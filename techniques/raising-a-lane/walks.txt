A `to` that names a storey — `x,z,y` — is what asks about the deck rather than the lane under it.
With no flight at all both of those answered `barrier +8`: the storey was a roof, not a route. With
one flight across the whole arm the lane answered `barrier +8` instead, because a player running it
the other way meets the flight's face. Two flights three wide on the arm's edges leave six blocks
of lane open between them, and all three walks come back clean.
