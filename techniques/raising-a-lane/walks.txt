A `to` that names a storey — `x,z,y` — is what asks about the deck rather than the lane under it.
Without the flight both of those answered `barrier +8`: the storey was a roof, not a route. The
first walk still reports one, because from the lane's foot the cheapest way to the arm's far end
is over the deck rather than under it, and the walk prices a climb a player pays for in blocks.
