# Blastworks: the author's second concept, finished

**Blastworks is a capture-the-wool board for two teams of sixteen, built in Python against pgmvox from the open
concept in the author's concept world.** The board is in `freeform/lib/boards/blastworks`, its world in
`maps/ctw/blastworks`. No studio was driven for it and nothing was written to the deployed studio.

## 1. What the concept held

**The open concept is a spawn, a box of bedrock with the wool in it, and the way between them.** Its signs say the
wool is blown into from the top and from the side through two doors of end stone, that a line of bedrock on the box
keeps its defenders off its top, and that redstone marks where a team may build. A stair up and a stair down lead
from the spawn, and from a platform a player drops onto a buildable void.

## 2. How the layout was reached

**The layout took five plans drawn as pictures, each answered by the author.** A plan in text could not be judged.
The corrections were about play, not look.

| The author's correction | What it taught |
|---|---|
| the spawn is the map's end | nothing goes behind it, not even a build zone |
| a one-block lip or gap between pieces | edges meet flush; a zone in line with a stair, not near it |
| a wool room reached through the enemy's spawn | a room is judged by who can walk to it, defenders included |
| the jump onto the Steg read as a gap | the arm is pasted onto the east block, no zone of its own |
| a tunnel in the arm | the tunnel is in the east block only |
| natural pieces as floating islands | natural ground is solid to the world's floor |

## 3. How it looks

**The author chose one grammar out of five and then trimmed it.** It kept diorite and spruce with quartz details.
Glowstone, iron blocks and a pitched roof were taken out, and gravel is used only on the surface. The pillars' caps
are four quartz stairs that close into one, and the spawn's house became a piece of the grammar set into the ground,
hollow, with the spawn on a 2x2 wool pad and the monument against the quartz post by its door.

**The concept's bedrock stands where the concept put it:** the box's walls to y 13, the line across its top, the
bridge's slab, and the east block's face over the void.

**The author's review caught a monument no one could use.** In the hall's corner it was closed on all four sides,
and inside the spawn's protection no block may be placed at all. It now stands open against a post, and its column
is left out of the protection. The wool room was furnished as a store, with nothing standing in front of a slit.
