# The rules switched off: what came out

**The rules were not what kept the studio's boards plain.** The same two briefs (`BRIEFS.md`) were built a third
time, by Sonnet 5.5 in about 25 minutes each, on a local studio running `Rules:Mode=minimal` (pgm-studio,
`docs/refusals.md` § *The minimal policy*): only a readable request, a map PGM loads, sound objectives, buildable
geometry and one thing never inside another still refuse. The boards are `exp-slatefold-min` and
`exp-abbeymoor-min`; their builders' accounts are `reports/exp-slatefold-min.md` and `reports/exp-abbeymoor-min.md`.

**Each finished board was then sent unchanged, as a dry run, to a studio with every rule on.** The logs are
`fullcheck-exp-*.txt` beside this file.

| Board | Full rules, dry run | What would have refused | Complaints in every mode |
|---|---|---|---|
| `exp-slatefold-min` | 200, accepted | nothing | 22 × EL1, WL10, WL11, WL18, FR6, FR9, G8, LN5, SK27, 2 × DR-TONE |
| `exp-abbeymoor-min` | 422, refused | 11 × SK13: the crypt's stair well and lane, ground laid back into a subtract | 3 × EL1, GO1, GO2, GO3, LN5, ST9 |

**On the source path the layout and walking rules already answer as complaints.** The Slatefold builder believed the
full rules would have pulled its wool rooms back from the front line; they would not have, because WL10, WL18 and FR9
arrive there as complaints today.

**What actually stops a builder is structural.** The one refusal was SK13, which is the column model: a column holds
one span of ground per layer, so an underground passage is a subtract with ground put back over it, and putting
ground back into a subtract is what SK13 refuses. The dressing also drops props without stopping the build: a
cottage inside a goal's clearance (OB19), boulders by a road (DR-ROAD, DR-KEEP), a cottage across a path (DR-CROSS).

**Without the play rules the objectives drift back to where the author said they should not be.** Both Slatefold wool
rooms stand on the front line, 8 blocks of walk from the crossing, which the author's first review named as wrong
for the studio's first Slatefold. Those rules belong in front of the author as measurements before the build.

**Two side findings from the builders' reports, not yet checked:** `follow: 100` on a strata stack used as `fill`
answered 200 with no complaint and painted plain stone; an edit that folded a shape's outline was stored once.
RQ1 still refuses under the minimal policy (checked).
