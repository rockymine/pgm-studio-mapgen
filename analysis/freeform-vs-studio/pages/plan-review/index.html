Plan Review · Abbeymoor

A prototype of how an agent's plan could reach the studio for review before anything is built: the places with their reasons, the routes, the numbers against their targets, and the sections, laid over the land. Click anything on the map or in the lists to read it and leave a note on it. Notes are stored for the agent to read. The data is the real pgmvox Abbeymoor plan, at the version it was first reviewed in and after.
