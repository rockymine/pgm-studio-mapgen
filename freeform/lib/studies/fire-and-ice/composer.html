Fire and Ice Composer

Five forms from the studies on one board: the fissure, the low somma, the caldera lake, the maar and the snowfield. Drag a form to move it, the round handle to turn it, the square handle to resize it; the board rebuilds when you let go. In the plan north is to the left and east is down. Every change rebuilds the board with the same arithmetic as compose.py, so what you see here is what gets built. When you like it, copy the layout and send it back.
