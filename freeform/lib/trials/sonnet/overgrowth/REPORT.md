# Overgrowth

## After the playtest

**The wood is a quarter of what it was.** The first build planted 26 trees on red's half, 52 on the board, and their crowns met into one roof. Red's half now holds 7 and the board 14, and none grows in the valley between the courts and the ziggurat. Leaf blocks fell from 13,104 to 4,906 and log blocks, the ziggurat's included, from 1,856 to 866.

**What is left stands on the fringe.** Trees may now grow only on the terraces behind the two gorges, where the absolute z is 28 or more, and at the back of each court, where x is -54 or less. The lanes the plan measured keep their four-wide margin as before. The bushes, ferns and flowers are held to the same fringe, so the valley floor is open grass and boulders.

**The walks improved with the canopy gone.** Spawn to spawn was 155 and is 149, and the places reached on foot came down from 14,066 to 12,942 because the leaf tops, which count as ground to the walk, are gone. The ziggurat's door is 78 away against 86, and the other measures moved by a few blocks. The studio's reader takes the map.xml as valid with no issues.
