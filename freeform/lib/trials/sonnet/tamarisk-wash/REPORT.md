# Tamarisk Wash

## After the playtest

**The well's foot is open into the cistern.** The well house's ring of smooth sandstone ran down to the cistern's floor and walled the shaft off from the chamber beside it, so the ladder led to a sealed bottom. A three-wide, three-high opening is cut in the ring on the cistern's side, over a level floor with no step, so a player has three blocks of headroom crossing it. The cistern's floor reaches the shaft's foot in 6 moves, in both halves, since blue's is the turn of red's.

**A catwalk now carries the ladder to the door.** The shaft's mouth was a hole to the cistern with the ladder's top over air. Three blocks of smooth sandstone lie across it from the doorway to the ladder, one wide, so the ladder has somewhere to step off.

**The ghats are rebuilt from full blocks and slabs.** The two diagonal ramps down the wash's wall carried some 75 stairs between them, cut across by the qanat's mouth, which opened a four-deep trench in the south ghat. Each cell of the ramp now takes a level in half blocks from the graded profile, held so that no cell sits more than half a block from its neighbour. Read back, the north ghat has 116 cells, 52 of them slabs, and the south 122 with 49, no stairs, a largest step of 0.5 and four or more blocks of headroom.

**The qanat's mouth moved north so the south ghat is whole.** It opened at z 30, under the middle of the south ghat. It now opens at z 23 and runs under the ghat's upper part with three or more blocks of rock over it, and a clearing at its mouth keeps the wash's boulders off it. Mouth to cistern is 60 moves where it was 55, mouth to the well's foot 70 against 67, inside the target of 75.

**The rest of the walks stand.** Spawn to the monuments is 51 and 49 on foot as before, and to the enemy's 169 and 176. The studio's reader takes the map.xml as valid with no issues.
