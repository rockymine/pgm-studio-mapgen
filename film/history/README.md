# The history of the AI-made maps: what is here

This folder is the written history of a year of AI-assisted PGM map making by rockymine, with Ruediger_LP and a run of
models, from September 2025 to November 2026. It was researched on 27 November 2026 in many passes while the author
added recollections; no film is being built from it yet. Everything in it keeps three voices apart: what the **record**
holds, what the **author recalls**, and what is **inference**.

## Reading order

1. **`HISTORY.md`** — the history itself: ten chronological chapters, then what counts as an AI map, the playtests,
   what the hand-built map shows, and what remains open. Start here.
2. **`timeline.md`** — one table of dated events, each with its source and its voice.
3. **`author-account-2026-11-27.md`** — the author's spoken account in fourteen instalments, summarised, with the
   note-taker's corrections. Later instalments and the corrections supersede earlier ones (the CORRECTION on Bridgid II
   above all).
4. **`research/12-who-decided-what.md`** — every map split into seven decisions, who decided each and what executed it;
   the categories of "AI map".
5. **`research/13-match-log-checked.md`** and **`research/15-test-evenings-from-the-zips.md`** — the server's match log
   and the maps sent for each test evening; read together for the playtests (`HISTORY.md` chapter 12 reconciles them).

## What is settled

- The first worlds the studio built, on 2 July 2026, were three test seeds; the squared-paper drawing is Base 2-Island,
  the author specified the bedrock box, and seeds began in that session (research/09).
- Bridgid II is its own hand-built map; Brittlebush III was never player-tested and its look is the grammar's
  (account, correction and 2; research/11, research/05).
- Kuusepuu CTC is an ordinary destroy map with a cake objective, credited to a model for the conversion only (account 6;
  research/11).
- The moving wool room is Wandering U, added to PrivateMaps on 6 August (research/10, research/15).
- The graph idea was not dropped in 23 minutes: a design document was replaced and `FannedGraph` stayed (research/08).
- Freeform began as an overnight experiment on 7 October from the author's "what if" message (sessions/00, sessions/01).
- The test evenings were event rotations the plugin did not report; six evenings fit, three of them Sundays (research/13,
  research/15, account 9).
- Monuments: obsidian capped at three blocks from 19 August; 27-block cubes exist in two stored worlds (research/14).
- The flat line-up map is Ashfall Scar, 250 × 190, confirmed by the author on a render; the quartz board is Quillon
  Saltworks (account 12; research/03).
- Alderfen Holm has four cores (sessions/08, account 11). No long destroy match exists in the log (research/13).

- The ice map was begun about 17 October and built over a few weekends (account 14); Hello World Micro's layout is Hello
  World's on the grid, drawn by the author (account 3, 14); the staff's quoted lines may be used as they are (account 14).
- The boards of the 9–10 October viewing were the new pgmvox boards (account 14); Hollowcrown is the most probable of the
  two-monument board, the other two are unnamed between candidates (`HISTORY.md` chapter 9).
- The pause was needed after a month of weekly testing and a tool wanting care; twenty maps a night was never the aim, and
  more maps will be built and tested (account 14).
- On 27 November the arcade boards were tested again, Lantern Pass among them; real gameplay is to be recorded the next day
  (account 14).

- The notes of 9–10 October are of a viewing by three people, the first feedback on the pgmvox boards, not a test; the
  last test evening stays 27 September. There they decided to pause because the maps were getting "too good" (account
  13; any link to the later freeze is inference, `HISTORY.md` chapter 9).

## What is open

Which of two candidates each are the flying capture-the-wool board (Lantern Karst, Brassmoor Works) and the mesa board
(Redwash Mesa, Hollow Mesa), and that Hollowcrown is the two-monument board; where the viewing took place. Aerie's unplayable XML, which the author
states and no record holds; whether
the 50-log oak is the generated tree; the briefs of the first fifteen, Heftfold and
Whinberry Ring; which model "Muse Spark" is (the author believes a Meta model). `HISTORY.md` chapter 14 and
`interview-questions.md` carry them.

## What was not read

- **Session starts that could not be paged to**: sessions 2, 4, 5, 7, 10 and 12 (their write-ups say so at the end).
  Session 5's brief is quoted from a turn result even though its start was not reached; session 14's first phase and
  session 1's middle were not read either.
- **Assistant text in most sessions**: the write-ups read the author's messages and turn-closing summaries, not the
  model's running text or tool output, except where they say otherwise.
- **The private maps repository (PrivateMaps)**: no clone could read it; only its commit feed in `#maps-changelog` and
  the zips the author supplied.
- Also not read: the raw conversations behind research/15 (quoted, not committed); PGM's source (so the tab list's
  "made by" was not checked); the studio's local database and scanned corpus output; several artifacts linked from the
  documents.
