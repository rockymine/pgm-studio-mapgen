# The first v0 prototype, as it runs today

Four screenshots of `https://v0-occ-mapmaking-tools-b63noa.vercel.app/`, the link in the author's message of
11 February 2025 (`../2025-02-11-message-to-samschaap.md`), taken on 28 November 2026 in a headless browser.

**The page is titled "Random Spanning Tree Visualization".** It asks for a grid width and height (10 × 10 by
default), "Nodes per Tree" and "Number of RSTs" (2, 3 or 4), and offers six ways to place the nodes: Random,
Symmetrical Vertical, Symmetrical Horizontal, Grid-Like, Radial Symmetry and Displaced Radial Symmetry. "Regenerate
Trees" draws each tree's nodes and edges in its own colour and fills the grid cells along the edges, which is the
shading "to make it more blocky" the message describes. "Download Image" saves the drawing.

**The pictures are today's output, not February 2025's.** Every press of the button draws new trees, and whether the
deployment changed after the message was sent is not known; the controls match the message's description.
