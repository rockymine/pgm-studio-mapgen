## What would have to be decided before building

1. **Whether there is a film at all, and when.** The author's decision; nothing below binds it.
2. **Whose words may be used.** The staff chat is cleared as it is (account §14). The voices of the 2 July recording
   (Ruediger, TTtie, samschaap, nuttydragon), the forum thread, the direct messages and arcadeboss's messages are not
   (`interview-questions.md`, "Everyone quoted").
3. **What the film's argument is.** The documented history supports at least two: the year as a line from a graph to a
   grammar, ending at a map built by hand; or the year as a question the community asked — who made this map — and
   still asks of that hand-built map. Choosing one decides which scenes lead.
4. **Which open facts must be settled first.** Those that a scene would state: the commit that carried the Base maps,
   where the October viewing took place, and whether the cancelled contest is Technodono's (`HISTORY.md` ch. 17).
5. **What gameplay to record, on which versions.** Several boards were played in versions later rebuilt (Sunspit,
   Hollowmarch, the model's Millrace), so a recording shows today's board unless an older world is restored.
6. **Form.** Whether the 2 July recording is the spine (it is the only footage of a test) or one scene among the rest.
