What is staged

The people

Dale, the Pitchman, the players and their names are invented. Their walking, falling, mining and chat messages are animated over the footage. The rejection in the cold open is fiction.

Two small edits

For the monument gag, one obsidian block is removed in the "mined" frames. The green build zone and the red gap are drawn from the map file's own coordinates.

The three-hour match

The rebuild loop is documented as possible "for as long as the match ran". No record of an actual three-hour match was found, so the clock is a joke.

The sets

The studio, the machine room and the awards stage are small block sets built for this film. The voices are synthetic and meant to be replaced.

Tone rules

Every joke lands on the AI, never on a nominee or a player-made map. The Machine stays cheerful and certain. The players play it straight.

Re-shoot it in game

To film these with real skins, load the world from the mapgen repository and stand where the camera stood. The teleport puts your eyes on the camera; set the field of view to match.
